Component × GenAI Role

Every component across both architectures, and exactly how much — if any — generative AI it uses. The pattern to notice: GenAI is confined to a single job, converting messy language into structured data. Everything else is deterministic.

The headline

Of eleven components across both systems, eight use no generative AI at all. GenAI appears at exactly three points — reading a date off an exception document (A·v2), pulling figures out of varied statement formats (B), and writing cross-period narrative (B) — and only the last of those needs our proprietary data. The expensive, deterministic work stays in SQL, which is cheaper, faster, and fully auditable.
